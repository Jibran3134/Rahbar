## Behavior and limits

Exact citation triples must exist in this request, duplicates/unused objects are rejected, and every nonempty prose sentence/line is treated as a claim needing an ending inline citation. The checker accepts citations before/after punctuation and protects decimals/common abbreviations. Complex headings, abbreviations, quoted passages or alternative formatting can trigger a conservative retry/fallback. Syntactic validity cannot establish that a claim follows from the cited text; full evaluator/source review and indexed-poison testing remain future work.

Validation failures get one retry with safe failure-category/citation feedback and the unchanged question/evidence. Transport/timeout failures do not retry. HTTP timeouts and a 512-token output cap bound provider behavior in the ordinary local path; no hard real-time wall-clock guarantee is asserted. Repeated invalid output returns a canned low-confidence uncited response. Provider false-groundedness responses are canonicalized to avoid leaking extra factual claims. The stub quotes actual retrieved source content and is validated identically.

Sessions/cache/readiness/final observability/evaluator/full-threshold calibration are not implemented by S11. In particular, /readyz is still the starter placeholder until S14, session_id is null, and .75 is a provisional demo profile. No production-readiness claim.
