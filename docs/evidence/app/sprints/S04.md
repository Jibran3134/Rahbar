## Limits and handoff

Margin position is first/last extracted line, not geometric PDF coordinates. Detection requires at least eight nonblank lines/page and at least three pages / 60% recurrence per edge. Vocabulary and punctuation/content guards are deliberately narrow. Short pages, infrequent or OCR-corrupted labels and unfamiliar running titles can remain. Repeated unnumbered institutional titles can be ambiguous on new corpora; inspect before extending these rules. Hard-hyphen ambiguity, uncorrected OCR errors and imperfect PDF table extraction remain explicit limitations. See PIPELINE.md for exact normalization rules.
